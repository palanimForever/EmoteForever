# EmoteForever Changelog

## Unreleased

## 1.2.0

### New
- Right-click while holding the key cancels the wheel without an emote.
- "Choose with a click" option: pressing the key opens the wheel and keeps it open, then you pick an emote with a click.

### Fixed
- Fixed position: the ring marking where you started (move back into it to cancel) could be hidden behind an emote slot.

## 1.1.0

### New
- Set the key for the wheel right on the settings page again: click the field and press a key or mouse button. It is the same key as in the game's key bindings.

## 1.0.0

### New
- Fixed position: the wheel can open at the same spot every time instead of at the mouse (/emf → "Fixed position"). "Move" lets you drag it where you like. Choosing still works with a flick of the mouse, wherever the cursor is. A small ring marks where you started: move back into it and release to cancel.

### Changed
- When the wheel stays open for a click, an emote only lights up while the mouse is over the wheel (a click elsewhere closes it).
- The key is now changed in the game's key bindings: "Change" in the settings takes you there.

### Fixed
- Searching in the game's options could show "EmoteForever has been blocked from an action only available to the Blizzard UI".

## 0.1.0-beta

### New
- Emote wheel: hold the middle mouse button, move the mouse towards an emote and release. A short tap keeps the wheel open so you can choose with a click.
- Your portrait sits in the center; a bronze pointer glides to the emote you aim at. Your target is shown below the wheel.
- 48 emotes with their own symbols, plus every other emote of the game.
- Settings (/emf): choose any key or mouse button, 4 to 12 slots, wheel size, minimap button.
- Arrange the wheel: click a slot, pick an emote from a searchable list. Shift-click tries an emote out.
- Minimap button: left-click for settings, right-click opens the wheel.
