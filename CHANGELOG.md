# EmoteForever Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta

### New
- Emote wheel: hold the middle mouse button, move the mouse towards an emote and release. A short tap keeps the wheel open so you can choose with a click.
- Your portrait sits in the center; a bronze pointer glides to the emote you aim at. Your target is shown below the wheel.
- 48 emotes with their own symbols, plus every other emote of the game.
- Settings (/emf): choose any key or mouse button, 4 to 12 slots, wheel size, minimap button.
- Arrange the wheel: click a slot, pick an emote from a searchable list. Shift-click tries an emote out.
- Minimap button: left-click for settings, right-click opens the wheel.
