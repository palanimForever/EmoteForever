# Description (paste from here)

![EmoteForever](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/palanimForever/EmoteForever/main/docs/images/gallery-hero.jpg)

**EmoteForever** is an emote wheel made for World of Warcraft: Forever. Hold a key, flick the mouse towards an emote and let go – your character waves, thanks, dances or cheers at your target. No typing, no macros.

## Highlights

- **Hold, aim, release** – the wheel opens right at your cursor
- **Right at your target** – the wheel shows who your emote goes to
- **48 emotes with their own symbols** – plus every other emote of the game
- **Your own wheel** – 4 to 12 slots, arranged in a searchable editor
- **Any key** – middle mouse button by default, or any key or mouse button you like
- **Fits the game** – bronze rings like Forever's unit frames, your portrait in the center

## Hold. Aim. Release.

Hold the middle mouse button and the wheel opens at your cursor, with your portrait in the center. Move towards an emote – a bronze pointer glides along – and let go. Prefer clicking? Tap the key briefly and the wheel stays open until you choose.

![Hold, aim, release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/palanimForever/EmoteForever/main/docs/images/gallery-hold-aim-release.jpg)

## Right at your target

Emotes go straight to whoever you have targeted. The wheel shows their name and portrait right below it, so you always know who gets the wave.

![Right at your target](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/palanimForever/EmoteForever/main/docs/images/gallery-target.jpg)

## Your own wheel

Choose 4 to 12 slots and put any emote on any slot: click a slot, then pick from a searchable list of every emote in the game. Shift-click an emote to try it out first. New slots start with popular emotes.

![Arrange the wheel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/palanimForever/EmoteForever/main/docs/images/gallery-editor.jpg)

## Usage

- **Hold your key, aim, release** – perform an emote
- **Tap your key** – keep the wheel open and choose with a click
- **/emf** – open settings · **/emf edit** – arrange the wheel
- **Minimap button** – left-click for settings, right-click opens the wheel

## FAQ

**My middle mouse button is already in use?** EmoteForever tells you and lets you pick another key in the settings (**/emf**).
**Can I use emotes that are not in the list of 48?** Yes – every emote of the game can go on the wheel; emotes without their own symbol get a neutral one.
**Where are the settings?** Options → AddOns → EmoteForever, or **/emf**.
